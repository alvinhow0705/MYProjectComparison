Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

MYPropertyComparison ("we", "us", "our") operates the website mypropertycomparison.com, a property comparison and enquiry service for new launch projects in Malaysia. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to handling your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Submit an enquiry or lead form on our website or through our advertisements (including Google lead forms) — such as your name and phone number, and where provided, your buying purpose, interest level, budget and preferred location;
  • Contact us via WhatsApp or other messaging channels;
  • Request materials such as e-brochures, floor plans, price lists or our property eBook.

We also collect certain information automatically when you use the site:

  • Device type (mobile or desktop), pages visited, and actions taken on the site (such as searches, filters used and projects viewed);
  • The source that referred you to our site (for example, an advertising campaign tag);
  • Analytics data collected through cookies and similar technologies via Google Analytics and Google Ads (see Section 5).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and send you the property information you requested (e.g. e-brochures, floor plans, price lists, eBooks) — typically via WhatsApp;
  • To connect you with a property advisor regarding projects you have expressed interest in;
  • To follow up with relevant property recommendations, updates and promotions;
  • To understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it;
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Lead and enquiry information is stored securely in systems operated by trusted service providers (including Google Workspace services such as Google Sheets). Access is limited to persons who need it to respond to your enquiry. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only with:

  • Authorised property consultants or developer sales teams, where necessary to fulfil your enquiry about a specific project;
  • Service providers who help us operate the website and store data (such as Google), under their respective security safeguards;
  • Authorities, where disclosure is required by law.

5. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand website usage and Google Ads (including conversion tracking and remarketing) to measure and improve our advertising. These services may set cookies or use similar technologies to collect information about your interaction with our site. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. You may also disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function fully.

6. Your Rights

Under the PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated data;
  • Withdraw your consent to our use of your data and request deletion, subject to legal requirements;
  • Opt out of receiving further marketing messages at any time — simply reply "STOP" or tell us via WhatsApp.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as developer virtual tours or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date shown above.
