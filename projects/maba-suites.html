The Development

A new address at the foot of an icon.

MABA Suitez is a collaboration between MABA and EXSIM, part of the EXSIM Group of Companies. It is a serviced-residence development in the heart of Kuala Lumpur, suited to both own-stay and investment.

  • Directly opposite Merdeka 118 — the tallest tower in Malaysia, second tallest in the world
  • Studio, 1+1 bedroom and 2-bedroom layouts
  • Roughly 500m to Merdeka MRT station (SBK17)
  • 118 Mall, Merdeka Stadium and Lalaport all within walking distance
  • Developed in collaboration with EXSIM Group of Companies
  • Indicative pricing from RM720,000, subject to change
