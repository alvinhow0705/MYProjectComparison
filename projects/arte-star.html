A transit address with two personalities

Arte Star is the newest project in Arte Corp's Arte series, sitting where Sungai Besi meets Salak South. Each tower takes its cue from a European city: The Regal draws on London, Milano on northern Italy.

What ties them together is the location. Salak Selatan station, with LRT, KTM and the upcoming MRT Circle Line, is a short walk away, and five expressways meet close by.
