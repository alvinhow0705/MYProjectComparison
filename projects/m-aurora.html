A new dawn on Old Klang Road

M Aurora takes its name and mood from the northern lights. It is Mah Sing's first project on Old Klang Road and the latest in its M Series, following the fully sold M Oscar in Sri Petaling.

The site covers 5.24 acres of freehold land, with 1,544 homes across two towers. Layouts run from compact 1+1 units for professionals to 4-bedroom homes for multigenerational families, and selected units offer flexible or dual-key configurations.

Questions, answered

Is M Aurora freehold?

Yes. The land tenure is freehold, with no restrictions in interest listed in the advertising particulars.

What layouts are there?

Five: Type A (1+1 bed, 1 bath, 556 sq ft), Type B (2 bed, 2 bath, 737 sq ft), Type C (3 bed with balcony, 2 bath, 858 sq ft), Type D (3 bed with balcony, 3 bath, 916 sq ft) and Type E (4 bed with balcony, 3 bath, 1,019 sq ft).

How much is a unit?

The developer advertises prices from RM339,000*. The approved selling price range in the permit is RM492,960 to RM856,560 across both towers. Ask an agent for the current price list by layout.

When is completion?

September 2030, according to the advertising and sales permit.

How many car parks come with a unit?

Between 1 and 3, depending on the unit.

What facilities are included?

46 named facilities, including a 50m lap pool, gym, yoga studio, co-working space, glamping dome, Aurora Garden, a multi-purpose hall for badminton and pickleball, and a Sky Lounge with a function space.

Who is the developer?

Major Land Development Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Mah Sing Group Berhad.
